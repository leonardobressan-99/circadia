About red light

!
Not a medical device

Red Light is for comfort and ambience. A phone screen is far too weak for red light therapy, and the app does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition.

Why red is gentler at night

Your eyes have a special light sensor (melanopsin) that tells your body clock it's daytime and holds back melatonin, the sleep hormone. It reacts most to blue light around 480 nm and barely at all to deep red.

MelatoninDeep red affects your body clock far less than white or blue light at the same brightness.
Night visionThe rods in your eyes barely respond to red, so your eyes stay adjusted to the dark. That's why astronomers and pilots use red light.

Screen light vs. therapy devices

Red light therapy (photobiomodulation) uses dedicated LED panels at 630–660 nm and invisible near-infrared at 810–850 nm, with much more power. A screen gives gentle ambient light, not a treatment dose.

How to use it

  1. 1
    Lower your screen brightnessDim is enough in a dark room. Turn off Night Shift and True Tone for the purest red.
  2. 2
    Place it 0.5–1 m awayPoint it at a wall or the ceiling for an even, indirect glow.
  3. 3
    Set a sleep timerThe light fades out over the last minute, and your session is saved to Stats.

What not to do

  • Don't use it as a treatment or instead of medical care.
  • Don't stare into the screen up close for long periods.
  • Avoid Breathe mode and very low brightness if you're sensitive to flicker or have photosensitive epilepsy.
  • Don't fall asleep with a bright screen beside your face. Use the timer.
  • Don't expect it to fix ongoing sleep problems. Talk to a doctor if they last.
